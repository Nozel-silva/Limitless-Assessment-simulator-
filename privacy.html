Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

👋 Who We Are

PrecisionTest is an online assessment practice platform designed to help Nigerian job seekers and applicants prepare for aptitude and numerical reasoning tests. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

📋 What We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our service:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and save your access
  • Usage data — pages visited and features used, collected anonymously
  • Device information — browser type and screen size for optimising your experience

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, or payment information through this platform.

🔐 How We Use Your Data

Your email address is used exclusively to:

  • Create and identify your PrecisionTest account
  • Allow you to sign back in without a password
  • Send occasional product updates (only if you opt in)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

🗄️ Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider with industry-standard encryption. Your email is stored in an access-controlled database and is never exposed publicly.

Session data (your login status) is stored locally in your browser and automatically expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage (not traditional cookies) to maintain your login session. This data stays on your device and is cleared when your session expires or you sign out. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

🧒 Children's Privacy

PrecisionTest is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with their email, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

✋ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by contacting us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of PrecisionTest after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
