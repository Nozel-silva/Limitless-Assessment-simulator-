Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

🇳🇬

NDPA 2023 Compliance. This Privacy Policy is prepared in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Bureau (NDPB). As a Nigerian platform serving Nigerian users, we are committed to full compliance with these regulations.

👋 Who We Are

PrecisionTest is an online assessment practice platform designed to help Nigerian job seekers and applicants prepare for aptitude and numerical reasoning tests. We act as a Data Controller under the NDPA 2023, meaning we determine how and why your personal data is processed.

We are committed to protecting your personal information, being transparent about how we use it, and giving you full control over it.

📋 What We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our service:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and allow you to sign in
  • Session data — stored locally in your browser to keep you logged in for up to 30 minutes
  • Usage data — pages visited and features used, collected anonymously to improve the platform
  • Device information — browser type and screen size for display optimisation

We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, financial information, or any sensitive personal data as defined under the NDPA 2023.

⚖️ Legal Basis for Processing

Under the NDPA 2023, we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — by entering your email and clicking "Continue", you consent to us storing and processing your email address
  • Contract performance — processing is necessary to provide the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — anonymous usage analytics help us improve the platform without affecting your rights

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us or requesting account deletion.

🔐 How We Use Your Data

Your email address is used exclusively to:

  • Create and identify your PrecisionTest account
  • Allow you to sign back in without a password
  • Send occasional product updates (only if you explicitly opt in)

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

🗄️ Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud infrastructure provider with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS encryption in transit. Access to your data is strictly controlled and limited to authorised personnel only.

Session data is stored in your browser's local storage and automatically expires after 30 minutes. It is never transmitted to third parties.

While we take all reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Bureau within 72 hours as required by law.

📅 Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary. Here is our retention schedule:

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Email address Until you request deletion Account identification
Session data (local storage) 30 minutes or until sign out Login session management
Anonymous usage data 12 months Platform improvement
Cookie consent record Until browser data is cleared Compliance record

To request deletion of your data at any time, contact us at the address below.

🍪 Local Storage & Cookies

We use browser local storage (not traditional tracking cookies) for two purposes:

  • Maintaining your 30-minute login session
  • Recording your cookie consent so we don't ask again

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics that identify you personally. You will be shown a consent notice the first time you visit. You can clear your local storage at any time through your browser settings.

🌍 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate PrecisionTest:

  • Supabase — database and backend infrastructure (stores your email). Privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy
  • Payment provider — for processing guide purchases. Their privacy policy applies to payment transactions

These providers are contractually required to handle your data in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

🧒 Children's Privacy

PrecisionTest is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has registered on the platform, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account without delay.

✋ Your Rights Under NDPA 2023

As a data subject under Nigerian law, you have the following rights:

✅ Right to Access Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
✏️ Right to Rectification Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
🗑️ Right to Erasure Request deletion of your account and all associated data
⛔ Right to Object Object to processing based on legitimate interests
📦 Right to Portability Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format
↩️ Right to Withdraw Consent Withdraw consent at any time without affecting past processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days as required by the NDPA 2023. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Bureau (NDPB) at ndpb.gov.ng.
